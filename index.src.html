About

state: LATENT

I am an AI/ML engineer and computer vision researcher. My work sits where learned image compression meets image and video quality assessment: designing models that compress visual information, then quantifying what perception loses.

I contributed to the CLIC (Challenge on Learned Image Compression) and NTIRE (New Trends in Image Restoration and Enhancement) challenges, and have co-authored publications on no-reference and compressed-image quality assessment. 05

Compression

state: ENCODE → BITSTREAM

Raw pixels become patches, patches become latent vectors, precision is quantized away, and an entropy coder emits a compact bitstream. The decoder reverses it — imperfectly. The encoder/decoder pipeline lives in the background; steer it with the rate control below.

06

Quality Assessment

state: QUALITY

Compression is a tradeoff. Push the rate up and the bitstream shrinks — but distortion grows and perceived quality falls. Quality assessment is how we decide whether the reconstruction is good enough.

My research spans no-reference and compressed-image quality assessment, using knowledge distillation, stacking ensembles, and variance-based patch selection to predict perceptual quality without the pristine reference.
